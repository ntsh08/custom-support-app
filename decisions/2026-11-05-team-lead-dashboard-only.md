## Problem
The Team Lead could need approval screens, case views and coaching flows. The brief asks for one screen, and time is short.

## Decision
Design only the Team Lead dashboard: how the operation is doing plus a list of items that need the lead (approvals, overdue courier replies, failed refunds, AI-spotted patterns, suspected false claims). No full flows behind the items. Case 4's approval appears as a dashboard item, not a designed approval flow.

## Why
Nitish: the brief asks for a single screen; we'll think again if there is time.

## Evidence
Brief wording ("Design a single screen").   Confidence: High

## Rejected options
- Full Team Lead flows (approval screen, case review, coaching) → beyond the brief and the 3-day timeline.

## Guardrails for the next designer

- Every item on it should still name the case it belongs to.
