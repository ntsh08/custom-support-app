# Linear's composition rules with one green accent, replacing Family and purple

## Problem
The first direction (Family-inspired, purple as the brand and AI colour) wasn't giving good results, and purple made the product look like generic AI output.

## Decision
- Follow Linear's composition rules: depth from greys, not effects; hairline borders; a 4px spacing grid; Inter at 13–14px.
- Use one green accent (#3A7C57), only on the logo, the primary button and the focus ring.
- The AI's own look is a green gradient border and label, not a separate brand colour.

## Why
Nitish: purple looks like AI slop, and the product shouldn't. The Family design system wasn't giving good results, so the design switched to a theme like Linear's. Green looks the least like AI slop, because very few AI products use it.

## Evidence
Type, structure, colour and icon rounds compared against Linear, Attio and Plain.   Confidence: Medium

## Rejected options
- Family-inspired look (pill buttons, big numbers) → not giving good results.
- Purple brand and AI colour → reads as AI slop.

## Guardrails for the next designer
- One accent, used rarely. Status chip colours are the only exception.
- Don't bring back purple as a brand colour.

## Revisit when
The product gets a real brand identity.
