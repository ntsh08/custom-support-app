## Problem
Secondary research needed "top 3 players" in AI customer support to learn how they handle the ticket journey and where AI helps.

## Decision
Study one market leader (Zendesk) and two AI-native companies (Sierra, Decagon).

## Why
Nitish chose "1 leader, 2 AI natives" over the suggested Zendesk/Intercom/Freshdesk and the all-AI-native options. A further reason was not stated.

## Evidence
Designer judgement.   Confidence: Medium

## Rejected options
- Zendesk, Intercom, Freshdesk → all incumbents; less signal on agent-first approaches.
- Sierra, Decagon, Intercom Fin → no incumbent baseline to compare against.

## Guardrails for the next designer
- Other tools (Gorgias, Plain, Intercom, Front) appear only as Mobbin UI references, not as the competitor analysis.

## Revisit when
Juspay names tools its merchants actually use for support.
