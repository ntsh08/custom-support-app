# Green is the one accent colour

- **Status:** Active
- **Type:** Visual
- **Decided by:** Nitish
- **Screens:** All
- **Link:** ../DESIGN.md
- **Supersedes:** none

## Problem
JusDesk needed one accent colour that wouldn't make it look like every other AI product or every other support desk.

## Decision
Green `#3A7C57` (hover `#2F6547`) is the only accent. It appears on the logo, the primary button and the focus ring, and on AI surfaces through the AI signature. It's never a fill, a section background or a selected state.

## Why
Nitish: green looks the least like AI slop, and the other customer support apps weren't using green, so it made sense to use it.

## Evidence
Comparison with the support desks studied (research/competitors.md, research/workflow research/).   Confidence: Medium

## Rejected options
- Purple → looks like AI slop.

## Guardrails for the next designer
- One accent, used rarely. Status chip colours are the only exception.

## Revisit when
The product gets a real brand identity.
