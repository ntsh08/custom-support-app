## How to maintain this log

Record activity, evidence, learning and consequence. Mark work as completed only when an artifact or conversation supports it. Link approved choices to decisions/ rather than presenting every research suggestion as a decision. Distinguish secondary research, participant reports, designer interpretation and tested findings. Do not claim research saturation or usability validation from one interview.
