What changed from the sketch below
1. Purple only on the one main action. Avatars, selected row and badges are neutral.
2. Queue uses plain text. Colour only when something is urgent ("40 min left").
3. The headline is the contradiction, not the price.
4. Evidence is shown: the courier's photo, the last delivery to this door, and the map.
5. AI is one plain line with a reason. No sparkle card.
6. Fewer boxes, thin dividers, and a Family-style confirm sheet before anything is final.
